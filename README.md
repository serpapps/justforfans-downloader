## Why JustForFans Downloader

JustForFans content is not one simple file type. Videos can be streamed in pieces, galleries need
expanding, and text posts live alongside media in the same feed. Saving content you have access to
usually means screen recording or manual page inspection.

JustForFans Downloader scans the feed you have open with your own session, converts streamed videos to
MP4 in the browser and saves images, galleries, text and playlists in an organized way.

## Features

- **Feed scanning**: finds videos, images, galleries and text posts on the creator feed you have open
- **Per-post buttons**: download a single video directly from the post
- **Stream conversion**: HLS and compatible DASH sources are converted to MP4 in the browser
- **Quality selector**: lists detected variants, highest first; MP4 is preferred over HLS when both exist
- **Gallery support**: expands multi-image posts so every image is saved
- **Playlist bulk download**: Bulk Saver saves every video in a playlist to the folder you choose
- **Download Visible**: grab all detected items on screen in one click
- **Right-click download**: context-menu option for videos
- **Organized files**: single downloads go to a `JustForFans` subfolder in Downloads
- **Private by design**: detection, conversion and saving run in your browser; files are not uploaded anywhere

## How to Download JustForFans Videos (Step-by-Step)

1. Install the extension (see [Installation Instructions](#installation-instructions)) and sign in with your email.
2. Sign in to JustFor.Fans and open a creator feed your account can view. Let the visible posts load.
3. Use the download button on a video post, or open the extension and click **Download** next to an item in the detected-media list.
4. To save a whole playlist, open the playlist tray on the creator's page, switch to the extension's **Bulk** tab and start the download. Bulk Saver saves the videos to the folder you choose, in a subfolder named after the playlist.

## Troubleshooting

**New posts are not found after scrolling**  
Scroll until posts are fully loaded, then rescan or reopen the popup. Detection works on posts currently loaded in the page.

**A download fails**  
Make sure you are signed in to JustFor.Fans with access to the post, then refresh and try again.

## Installation Instructions

1. Download the ZIP for your browser from the [latest release](https://github.com/serpapps/justforfans-downloader/releases/latest) (for example `justforfans-downloader-chrome.zip`).
2. Unzip it. You get a folder that contains `manifest.json`.
3. Open your browser's extensions page: `chrome://extensions` (Chrome), `edge://extensions` (Edge), `brave://extensions` (Brave), `opera://extensions` (Opera), `whale://extensions` (Whale) or `browser://extensions` (Yandex).
4. Turn on **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the unzipped folder.
6. Pin the extension, click its icon, and sign in with your email and the one-time code we send you.

Prefer a guided setup? The [product page](https://serp.ly/justforfans-downloader?via=github) walks you through the same steps.

[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/serpapps/justforfans-downloader/releases/latest) and load it the same way.

## FAQ

### How do I download videos from JustForFans?

Open a creator feed on JustFor.Fans, let visible posts load, then use the per-post download button on a video or the **Download** button next to each item in the extension's detected-media list.

### Can I download a creator's whole JustForFans playlist?

Yes. Open the playlist tray on the creator's page, switch to the extension's **Bulk** tab and start the download. Bulk Saver saves every video in the playlist to the folder you choose, in a subfolder named after the playlist.

### What content types are supported?

Videos, images (including gallery and carousel sets) and text posts from feed content you can already view.

### Can I choose video quality?

Yes. The quality selector lists detected variants sorted highest first. MP4 variants are preferred over HLS where both exist.

### Does it work with gay creators on JustFor.Fans?

Yes. Many JustFor.Fans creators are gay and bi men, and the downloader works the same on every creator page your account can view: videos, photos and galleries, text posts and playlists.

### Does it work with subscriber-only posts?

It works with posts your account already has access to. It does not unlock content you have not paid for.

### Can it download live streams?

No. It is built for feed and post content, not live-stream recording.

### Where are downloaded files saved?

Single downloads are saved to a `JustForFans` subfolder in your browser's Downloads folder. Playlist downloads go to the folder you choose in Bulk Saver.

### Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Whale and Yandex on Windows, macOS and Linux. Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers are not supported.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get **3 free downloads a week** with no credit card. A **subscription** gives unlimited downloads, with updates included.
